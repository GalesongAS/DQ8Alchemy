# DQ8Alchemy

[Open the recipe finder](https://galesongas.github.io/DQ8Alchemy/)

Pick the ingredients in your Dragon Quest VIII bag to see what you can make in the Alchemy Pot and what you’re missing. Switch on Masterpiece Reborn to see the mod’s recipes.

![Dragon Quest VIII recipe finder showing a recipe ready to brew](docs/preview.png)

Filter recipes by item type or character, and star favorites to find them again.

An unofficial fan project. Dragon Quest VIII and its imagery belong to their respective rights holders.
